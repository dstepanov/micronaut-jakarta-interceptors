`jakarta.interceptor.InvocationContext` is what an interceptor method is given. Its behaviour follows the
specification:

|===
| Method | Behaviour

| `getTarget()`
| The intercepted instance. `null` inside an `@AroundConstruct` method until it has proceeded.

| `getMethod()`
| The intercepted method for an `@AroundInvoke` method, `null` for a lifecycle callback and for a constructor.

| `getConstructor()`
| The constructor of the target class inside an `@AroundConstruct` method, `null` otherwise.

| `getTimer()`
| The schedule of a method the scheduler invokes, as a api:interceptor.runtime.ScheduledTimer[]; `null` for
anything else. See <<timeout,Timeout Methods>>.

| `getParameters()`
| The arguments of the invocation, as a copy. Fails with an `IllegalStateException` for a lifecycle callback.

| `setParameters(Object[])`
| Replaces the arguments the rest of the chain, and finally the intercepted element, is invoked with. The count and
the types are checked.

| `getContextData()`
| A map shared by every interceptor of one invocation, and by any other Micronaut advice on the same invocation.

| `proceed()`
| Continues to the next interceptor of the chain and finally to the intercepted element.

| `getInterceptorBindings()`
| The binding annotations in effect on the intercepted element, as annotation instances.
|===

An interceptor that returns without proceeding keeps the rest of the chain, and the intercepted element, from
running. For a lifecycle callback that is all it does: the bean is neither replaced nor discarded.

An exception thrown by an interceptor method reaches the caller unchanged, checked exceptions included: the
intercepted method is what the caller sees, and it declares them.

`getParameters()` shows the arguments the intercepted element declares, and nothing else. A Kotlin suspending
function is compiled with a continuation of its own after them; that argument belongs to the language and is
neither shown nor asked for.
